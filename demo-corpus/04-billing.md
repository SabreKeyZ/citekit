# Billing

Lumen Notes sells three workspace plans. Charges are monthly, in USD, and
prorated when a seat is added mid-cycle.

## Free

The Free plan includes **50 notes** per workspace, one seat, and extractive
ask only. This cap conflicts with the marketing overview, which still says
100 notes. Billing is the system of record for invoices; marketing is not.

Private notes count toward the Free cap. When the cap is reached, new notes
are blocked and existing private notes become read-only until the workspace
upgrades or deletes a note.

## Pro

Pro costs $12 per seat per month. It includes unlimited notes, generate-mode
answers, and a shared retrieval index. A workspace may add seats from the
billing portal. Removing a seat takes effect at the next renewal.

## Team

Team adds SSO, audit exports, and a 90-day evidence board history. Price is
$28 per seat per month with a five-seat minimum.
